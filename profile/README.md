## Harnesses

Four harnesses. Different jobs. They share the Radi operating model. They are not interchangeable.

- **[KlarForm](https://www.radilabs.com/klarform/)** (identity and positioning) — structured business artifacts from founder and company material.
- **[Vulcan](https://www.radilabs.com/vulcan/)** (operations and infrastructure) — recommendations, decisions and action plans from operational context.
- **[Daedalus](https://www.radilabs.com/daedalus/)** (software production) — working software from product intent and repository state.
- **[4wd Scout](https://www.radilabs.com/4wd-scout/)** (opportunity discovery) — discovery and assessment from company and market material.

See [radilabs.com/harnesses](https://www.radilabs.com/harnesses/) for details.

## Radilabs, Radi and Forthscale

These names are not interchangeable. One is the company, one is the technology, and one is the legal operating side that applies the work.

- **Radilabs** — the company and lab. It researches and builds the harnesses, methods and reusable technology.
- **Radi** — the underlying technology and factory concept those systems are built with.
- **Forthscale Systems Limited** — the legal operating company. It applies the approach to infrastructure, operations and client systems.
